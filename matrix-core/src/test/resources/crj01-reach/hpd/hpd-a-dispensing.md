# The unit-dose dispensing workflow of a hospital pharmacy

A dispensing cabinet in a hospital pharmacy stores the unit doses of the medicines that the ward asks for.

The unit-dose dispensing process of a hospital requires a prescription that names the medicine and its dose.

A barcode on a unit dose of a medicine allows the cabinet to check that medicine against the prescription of the ward.

The picking list of a ward contains one line for every medicine that the ward needs during the day.

A picking machine in a hospital pharmacy takes the unit dose from the cabinet and records that removal in the log.

The dispensing log of a hospital pharmacy provides the record of every unit dose that left the cabinet.

A unit dose that does not match the barcode of its medicine requires a manual check before the ward receives it.

An unrecorded unit dose in a hospital pharmacy reduces the ability of the pharmacy to account for the medicines in its cabinets.

A unit dose in a hospital pharmacy has a label that the ward checks before the dose reaches the patient.

A unit dose with a label that the printer produced incorrectly requires a manual check before the ward receives it.
