# Instrumentation for a battery laboratory

A thermocouple glued to the surface of a cell measures the external temperature of the cell.

A humidity sensor installed in a test chamber observes the moisture of the air around the cell.

A vibration shaker produces the mechanical excitation that a vehicle delivers to a battery pack.

An ultrasonic probe reveals the delamination of the electrode stack of a prismatic cell.

A reference electrode placed next to a half cell measures the potential of the working electrode directly.

A potentiostat provides a controlled current to a laboratory cell and measures its terminal voltage.

An environmental chamber controls the air temperature and the relative humidity around the specimen.

A data logger samples the sensor channels of a test bench at a fixed rate and stores them on a local disk.

A reference thermometer placed in the coolant stream measures the temperature the loop actually carries.

A fume hood captures the gaseous products released by a cell that vents during an abuse test.

A calorimeter measures the total energy a laboratory cell releases during a complete discharge.

A barcode reader provides the identity of the specimen that a technician loads into the test bench.
