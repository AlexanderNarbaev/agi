## CRITICAL BUG FIXED

BitLinearTrainer.flippedTable() had a logic bug — it was clearing cells
where bit `flippedBit` was 0, but this didn't implement the actual
"flip bit" semantics that findBestFlip chose.

### Before fix
- Training reported flips (e.g., "trained on pair → N neurons flipped")
- But the chain's neurons never actually changed
- Neuron's hash/cardinality were identical before and after training

### After fix
- Training actually changes neurons
- Verified: neurons 50, 100, 200 changed after training
- Hash differences confirmed in /v1/chain-debug/neuron responses
- Log now reports "N flipped, N written, M actually changed" where M > 0
