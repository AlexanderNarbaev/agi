## FINDINGS

### Positive
1. Write-back is REAL — neurons' hashes changed after training
2. Generation output CHANGES after training (some tokens come/go)
3. Chat QA retrieval still works (unaffected)
4. Chain continues to respond to training (no plateaus)

### Negative
1. **OVERFITTING**: After 1000 exposures, all prompts converge to the same
   Korean+French+Clement output. The chain has learned a "frequent" pattern
   from the corpus and doesn't differentiate between prompts.
2. **Training is too aggressive**: ~8000 of 21960 neurons flipped per pair.
   Need a learning rate cap or per-pair neuron limit.

### Lessons
- The training mechanism is correct (neurons change, generation changes)
- BUT the training is too aggressive, causing mode collapse
- Next step: add a `maxFlipsPerPair` parameter (e.g., 100) to limit
  per-pair changes and prevent overfitting
