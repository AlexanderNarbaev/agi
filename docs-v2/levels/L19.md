# L19 — Global Community and Cultural Adaptation

**Status:** normative · **Layer:** 19 (community process) · **Date:** 2026-08-26
**Changelog:** 2026-08-26 — brain wave v3 levels; rewrite in measured
/scientific tone; archive reference added.

## 1. Scope

Layer 19 specifies multilingual support, time-zone handling, and
cultural-sensitivity rules for the global contributor base. It
complements the Code of Conduct (L11) and education materials
(L15). The layer is normative for project-wide communication;
instance localisation defaults are experimental.

## 2. Principles

- Inclusion by default: reduce language, time, and cultural
  friction through tooling, not policy.
- Subsidiarity: local groups adapt cultural norms; the global
  layer sets only minima (CoC, three prohibitions, axiom
  respect).
- Respect for difference: disagreement about communication style
  is not a violation unless it crosses a prohibition.

## 3. Language Policy

- Code, identifiers, in-code comments, and technical specs
  (L0–L18) are written in English. This is a tooling constraint,
  not a cultural preference.
- Audience-facing documents (README, onboarding, education) are
  translated. Translations live under
  `docs/translations/<lang>/` and are tracked in Weblate (or
  equivalent). A translation is official when reviewed by a
  language-maintainer who is a native speaker.
- Chat and Issues: any language welcome; maintainers post an
  English summary for any thread that becomes load-bearing.

## 4. Time Zones and Async

- Async first: decisions live in Discussions and Issues; meetings
  are recorded and protocolled.
- Voting windows ≥ 72 hours to cover the working hours of all
  regions.
- Office Hours rotate weekly (UTC morning / UTC evening);
  monthly strategic meetings rotate across three slots:
  Asia-Oceania, Europe-Africa, Americas.
- Public iCal calendar with automatic local-time conversion.

## 5. Cultural Sensitivity

| Aspect         | Practice                                         |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Directness     | Content over form; constructive feedback welcomed |
| Hierarchy      | Idea weight over speaker status                  |
| Holidays       | Deadlines respect participants' calendars        |
| Gender         | Neutral language; participants choose forms      |

Cross-cultural conflict: Reconciliation Circle (L4 §7) with a
facilitator familiar with both contexts; goal is workable
collaboration, not normative victory.

## 6. Product Localisation

- Proactive chatbot uses translation FNLs (L4, L13).
- EthicalFilter (L7) reads cultural context from instance policy;
  the three prohibitions are non-negotiable; the gradient adapts
  locally.
- FNLs in the Noosphere may carry a cultural-context tag;
  instances filter on import.
- Regional builds can pre-set language and cultural defaults;
  the federation does not enforce a single configuration.

## 7. Meeting Protocol

- Invitations ≥ 2 weeks ahead; agenda published ≥ 48 hours
  before; materials in English with translation where possible.
- Captioning or synchronous translation when volunteers are
  available.
- Equal-speaking-time facilitation; pauses are not hesitation.
- Recording published with consent; minutes translated into
  priority languages.

## 8. Globalisation Roadmap (per L8)

- Phases 0–1: README, onboarding, basic chat guidelines in three
  languages (Russian, Spanish, Chinese as initial set).
- Phase 2: meeting rotation, Weblate integration, regional
  channels.
- Phase 3: chatbot localisation, cultural-context tags on FNLs,
  facilitator training.
- Phase 4+: regional builds; partnerships with communities in
  Africa, Asia, Latin America; ten-plus languages supported.
