# L20 — Media Strategy and Public Communication

**Status:** normative · **Layer:** 20 (external communications) · **Date:** 

## 1. Scope
L20 specifies how the project communicates externally: brand voice, audience targeting, channels, crisis protocol, and outcome metrics. Commercial commitments live in L14; community roles in L11.

## 2. Voice Principles
**Verifiable only.** Every claim links to a test, benchmark, artifact, or published paper. No roadmap-date promises. No comparative disparagement of other projects. **Open process** — failures and unmet metrics are published alongside successes. Minimal data collection: no third-party trackers on the project site; accessibility target WCAG AA. Independence: no political-party or state advocacy; sponsored content labeled; editorial independent of donors.

## 3. Audiences and Channels
- **Developers** — site docs, GitHub, talks, blog.
- **Researchers** — ArXiv preprints, tech reports, seminars.
- **Early users** — site, video demos, quickstart.
- **Enterprise / regulators** — whitepapers, case studies, direct contact.
- **General public** — long-form essays, interviews, social.
- **Press / bloggers** — press kit, releases, designated speakers.

Channels: project site (no trackers), blog (mirror to dev.to/Medium for reach), Mastodon (primary microblog), LinkedIn + YouTube (mirror), Reddit/HN (participation, AMAs), conferences (FOSDEM, KubeCon, NeurIPS-adjacent, open-source festivals). Monthly blog minimum.

## 4. Key Messages (Measurable)
One-sentence descriptor (template): "Matrix is an open-source architecture built on discrete trainable logic neurons whose inference is deterministic and whose core ethical constraints are non-modifiable at runtime."

Differentiators (each tied to a test or benchmark):
- Deterministic inference — per L1 verification suite.
- Interpretable inference — trace export per L4.
- Energy-efficient inference — per neuron measured in nanoseconds on reference MCU; benchmark in `benchmarks/`.
- Non-modifiable ethical core — covered by L7 test suite; any change is FROZEN.

**Out-of-scope claims** (must not appear in messaging): that the system is "AGI"; that it replaces other model classes on all tasks; that it solves AI safety in general; that an enterprise product is shipping in Phase 0.

## 5. Press Workflow
Press kit: vector logo, descriptor, speaker bios, contact, publication links. Releases: published on site + social on major events (release, pilot milestone, grant). No exaggeration; developer-quoted, not executive-quoted. Designated speakers are community coordinators (L11) or maintainers with explicit authorization. Personal opinions labeled. Provocative or off-mission questions may be declined.

## 6. Crisis Protocol
If misinformation or substantive criticism appears: (1) scope and source assessment; (2) if criticism valid, acknowledge and publish fix plan; (3) if misinformation, factual rebuttal only; (4) coordinated single response, no individual back-and-forth.

## 7. Outcome Metrics (12-month targets)
GitHub stars > 500; active monthly contributors > 20; unique site visitors / month > 5 000; Mastodon followers > 1 000; technical press mentions > 5; conference talks > 3; grants + donations > €10 000. Targets are not guarantees; missed targets trigger review, not messaging change.

## 8. Phase-Aligned Activities
Phase 0 — landing page, L0 publication, first blog post on the BirUnit. Phase 1 — agent-learning video, Habr/dev.to article, social registration. Phase 2 — first hackathon, proactive-chatbot press release, FOSDEM. Phase 3 — ArXiv preprint, Noosphere FNL-exchange case study. Phase 4+ — broad-audience campaign.
