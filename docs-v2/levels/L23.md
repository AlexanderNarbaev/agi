# L23 — Corporate Engagement

**Status:** normative · **Layer:** 23 (corporate relations) · **Date:** 2026-08-26
**Changelog:** 2026-08-26 — brain wave v4 levels; rewrite in measured/scientific tone; capture-prevention controls made explicit; archive reference added.

## 1. Scope
L23 specifies forms of corporate participation (user, contributor, sponsor, partner, commercial licensee), the ethical audit, capture-prevention controls, fork policy, and the public registry. Licensing lives in L12; business model in L14.

## 2. Principles
**Openness without capitulation** — license, code visibility, and ethical restrictions are not negotiable for sponsorship. **Transparency** — every corporate relationship is recorded in the public registry; hidden influence prohibited. **Ethical gate** — any company whose primary business is incompatible with the Three Prohibitions fails the L7 audit. **Capture resistance** — distributed governance + license + patent grant-back prevent single-firm dominance.

## 3. Participation Forms
- **User** — uses Matrix in products under AGPLv3; publishes derivative source on distribution.
- **Contributor** — company employees contribute under their individual capacity; no voting rights proportional to employer.
- **Sponsor** — financial / infrastructure contribution. Levels: Bronze $500/mo (logo), Silver $2 000 (releases), Gold $5 000 (case study + strategic meetings, non-voting), Platinum $15 000+ (managed services + architect consult). Sponsorship confers no vote in technical or ethical decisions.
- **Partner** — strategic collaboration, joint development, open hardware / ROS 2 gateways, etc. Advisory voice; signs MOU with ethical clauses; cannot dictate roadmap.
- **Commercial Licensee** — proprietary use under commercial license (L14), royalty / fixed fee to community fund; ethical audit mandatory.

## 4. Ethical Audit
Required for partners, commercial licensees, and Gold and above. Criteria: primary business line (compatibility with Three Prohibitions), customer base, supply chain (forced labour, conflict minerals), transparency, public record on human rights / environment / corruption. Process: application + questionnaire → Ethics Commission (3 maintainers + 1 coordinator) → consensus decision, appealable to Maintainer Council. Renewal every 2 years or on material business change. Commission composition is published; ≥ 1/3 rotates annually; decisions public with confidential fields redacted.

## 5. Capture Prevention
Documented risks and countermeasures:
- **Hiring key developers** → distributed governance (L11); AGPLv3 prevents closed forks.
- **Financial pressure** → sponsorship carries no vote; consensus among maintainers, not budget holders.
- **Fork creation** → AGPLv3 obliges fork to publish source; ethical restrictions remain; community competes on quality.
- **Patent suit** → patent grant-back (L12 §3.1); initiation of patent action terminates participation.

Concentration limits: no single employer of > 20% of maintainers (recommended); single funding source < 30% of community budget (target). Coordinators actively diversify.

## 6. Fork Policy
Forks are legal and ethical under open-source norms. The project does not obstruct forks, but: the original project retains name, brand, and Noosphere registry; forks may not use the "Matrix" trademark without permission; ethical restrictions survive the fork. Healthy competition welcomed; ethics-circumvention forks are publicly disowned and excluded from Noosphere.

## 7. Registry and Application
Public `CORPORATE_PARTICIPANTS.md` (in repo): company, participation type, audit status (date), start date, contact. Maintained by community coordinators. Application flow: read L0 + L12 + L23 → submit form (`/corporate/apply`) with company, contact, desired form, intended use → for Partner / Licensee, ethical questionnaire → coordinator reply within 5 business days → on approval, sign MOU or license.

## 8. Conflict Handling
- **Ethics breach by participant** — commission review → suspension + cure, or revocation + public statement + registry removal.
- **Pressure on community** — coordinator escalates to maintainers; public statement; partnership termination if confirmed.
- **Maintainer conflict of interest** — declared; recused from affected vote.

## 9. Phase-Aligned Targets
Phase 0 — registry live; first Bronze sponsor. Phase 1 — first Contributor company; first case study. Phase 2 — first Partner; first commercial license. Phase 3 — diversified sponsor base (no source > 30%); first Platinum sponsor. Phase 4+ — multi-region partnerships; annual sponsor report.
