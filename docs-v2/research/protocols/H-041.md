# EXP-041 preregistration

## Hypothesis (H-041)
Offline dream-replay beats online retention on F1 forget/remember.
ΔF1 ≥ 0.05 in favour of REM arm (synthetic-scope).

## Setup
- 200 episodes split 80/20 train/holdout
- Two arms: online-only (no REM) vs online+REM
- "Forget" = episode present in train but predicted wrong on holdout
- "Remember" = episode correctly predicted on holdout
- F1 = 2·P·R / (P+R)
- 5 seeds

## Gate
- ΔF1 ≥ 0.05 in favour of REM arm
