Architecture Decision Records (6)

Why the architecture is what it is

6 documents · normative
ADR-001MATRIX vs RAG system roles

MATRIX is the deterministic kernel; RAG is the untrusted peripheral. Each does what it is good at.

ADR-002BIR as canonical representation

Why three equivalent forms instead of one. Capacity, evaluation cost, verifiability trade-offs.

ADR-003Tsetlin producers over living mechanisms

Why Tsetlin automata, not gradient-based learning, for BIR producers.

ADR-004Cauldron budget scheduling

Why Levin 2⁻ˡ schedule, not uniform, for candidate budget allocation.

ADR-005Three-valued gate verdicts

Why gates return ALLOW/DENY/UNDECIDED, not boolean. How undecided propagates to higher levels.

ADR-006LLM and external stores are not dependencies

MATRIX does not depend on an LLM or external datastore. They are peripheral services verified at the boundary.
