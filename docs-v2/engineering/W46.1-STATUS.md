### The one production change — a defect repair, and the owner's own words

`io.matrix.api.CanonicalEntryPoint` attributed `io.matrix.brain.BirBrainCycle` to the
**epistemic application**. The constant pool of the current build places it in **chain B**
(reached from `TrueMindCycle` and `PersistentMind`, and from the unmounted `BrainHttpServer`)
— **no chain A route reaches it**. The declaration's field records the chain whose closure
reaches the class, so the attribution was wrong. It is corrected, the note states the truth,
and a new test asserts the corrected attribution.

**The scope was kept narrow on purpose.** The defect sits inside a declaration that the W46
directive protects as a serving-topology record; the correction changes no runtime behaviour,
no contract and no boundary. The **writer-lock** defect (D4-a, the symlinked path alias) is
**not** fixed here — it touches the database-ownership enforcement, which the directive
protects until the owner approves. It is queued as TASK-W46-4.

## 2. The decisions this phase made, and their answers

Three subagent questions were answered by the coordinator, and the answers are recorded in
the documents themselves.

**The protocol's classifications were rewritten to the directive's exact wording.** The
worker had reconstructed thirteen task classes, four risk levels and six resource classes
of their own. The directive's enumerations are normative, and the file now carries them
verbatim — **twelve** task classes (not thirteen: the directive lists twelve), the four risk
levels `low · medium · high · critical`, and the six resource classes
`light · medium · heavy · GPU · native-build · external-service-dependent`. The worker's
operational content — the *tell* for each class, the verification each class may run, the
ceiling each resource class carries — was kept, because it is what makes the classification
decidable.

**The new prefixes in the decision register are confirmed.** `ND-` for the normative
decisions of `docs-v2/vision/CONCEPT-CORRECTIONS.md` (they are decisions, and no document
names a decider for them) and `CD-W42-nn` for the W42 coordinator decisions (the wave in the <!-- no-id: the family prefix, not an identifier -->
id, so that a W42 id can never be confused with a W45 one). The rule — **an id is minted once
per decision, a bare `D` is never used, and a decision declared in two documents is
registered once** — is what the register states.

**The duplicate-identifier problem is now visible.** The repository's `D1`/`D-001` means
**five different decisions** in five different documents. The register resolves it and says
so; the underlying collisions are not renamed in place, because the historical documents are
evidence.

**Three new questions and one new risk entered the registers**, raised by the audit and by
the work:

| the id | the question | the owner decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Q-W46-16** | the fifteenth TLA+ specification is neither gated nor registered — move it into `formal/` and gate it, or withdraw it? | **yes** |
| **Q-W46-17** | `docs-v2/quality/OPEN-DECISIONS.md` contradicts itself — the top section declares D1–D8 closed while the bodies read as open | no |

## 5. The checkpoint

**W46.1 is complete.** The repository now has a canonical set of eleven documents, one
subject per document, a machine-readable capability registry that a validator can check, a
test catalogue whose baselines are commit-specific, and a protocol that tells an agent what
it may change without asking.

**The acceptance test of the structure is in `PROJECT-STATE.md` §8: the mission's thirteen
questions, and the document that answers each.** A new agent reads five files — the project
state, the roadmap, the decision register, the open questions, and the subsystem it is about
to change — and does not read the history unless the task changes an architectural
assumption.

**W46.2 (the reconciliation) is the next phase, and it is where the broad documentation
changes live.** It repairs the broken references, rewrites the root README, reconciles the
index, resolves the native-image contradiction, and classifies the historical material. It
waits for the owner's review of the W46.0 findings and the answers to Q-W46-01, Q-W46-02,
Q-W46-03, Q-W46-07 and Q-W46-13.
