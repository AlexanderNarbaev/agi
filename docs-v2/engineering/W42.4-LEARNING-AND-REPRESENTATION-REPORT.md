## 1. The exercise selector

The mission's §10.1 asks for "an exercise generator and a learner practice
loop", and for the system to "suggest what to learn next, considering the
learner's current state and gaps". RECON-W41 had neither.

**The rules are in the order of what a learner needs first:**

1. an item of a dimension that has **not reached competence** — the weakest
   dimension first;
2. an item of a concept whose **prerequisites are missing** — the gap is
   upstream of every question about the concept;
3. the **held-out** item of a weak dimension — the independent test, which
   measures rather than rehearses;
4. otherwise the next item the learner has not attempted.

**Nothing is invented.** The selector chooses among the items the dataset
holds; it does not write a question and it does not report a score. The
mission's §5.4 forbids promoting generated content to trusted knowledge,
and a generated question the learner is graded on would be exactly that.

*Live:* `GET /workspaces/{id}/exercises/next` →
`"the dimension has not reached competence yet"` →
*"Explain in your own words how photosynthesis and cellular respiration
relate to each other."*

## 2. The delayed retention

The mission's §7.4 asks for "delayed retrieval, after a meaningful
interval", and §7.1 makes the lifecycle revisable: a previously mastered
capability may return to `PRACTICE` when the retention fails.

The dataset holds **no item of the `RETENTION` dimension at all** — so the
dimension had no criterion and could never be anything but `UNKNOWN`. The
mission's delayed retention was measured only by the teach-back experiment's
delayed phase, and not by the learning state at all.

**The retention evidence is the learner's performance before the most recent
session**: the outcomes of the sessions that came before it. The learner has
practised since, and what the earlier performance says about the material now
is the retention. The delay is a property of the **data** — the session
boundary — and not of a clock, which the CONSTITUTION's first article
forbids in a decision path.

## 3. The representation composer

The mission's §10.1 asks that "the system creates a representation for a
new concept", and the first practical objective says the system must
"improve its knowledge representation".

A concept that came from a document had claims and evidence but **no
authored account** — so the system's answer about it was the claim itself,
which is not an explanation.

**The composer does not write prose.** It assembles the account the document
already contains:

- the **simplest** claim about the concept is the introductory account;
- the claims that **relate the concept to another** are the intermediate one;
- the claims that say **what causes what** are the advanced one;
- the **quantitative** claims are the mechanistic one.

**A level is written only with claims the lower level does not have.** A
level that repeats the one below it is a second label on the same sentence,
and the reader would learn nothing from it. A concept with one simple claim
therefore gets one account — and that is the honest outcome, not a gap. When
the reader asks for a level the workspace does not hold, the answer **says
so**: *"This workspace has no mechanistic account of Glycolysis; the answer
uses the introductory one."*

The account also records **what it leaves out** — the claims of the concept
that the level does not include — so a reader can see the shape of the
account and not only its contents.

**The first version of the composer used a length test** to decide whether a
claim related its concept to another, and it called a long sentence about
water a structural account. The check is now against the other concepts'
names.

## 4. The claim extractor's verbs

The composer only saw what the extractor gave it, and the extractor was
losing sentences: "Water boils at one hundred degrees" was not a claim,
because `boil` was not in the verb list, and the `-es` folding turned
"freezes" into "freez" while the list held "freeze".

The verb list grew by the verbs of a physics, chemistry and biology
statement (142 verbs), and the `-es` folding now tries both stems.

## 5. The objective, demonstrated end to end

The mission's first practical objective, run against the live application
on port 9095:

| the step | the evidence |
|---|---|
| **acquire a concept from an actual document** | `POST /workspaces/photosynthesis/documents` → 4 segments, 7 claims, 5 concepts, **6 representations** |
| **explain it using its own stored knowledge** | the answer to *"What is the Krebs cycle?"* begins **"The Krebs cycle is the second stage of cellular respiration."** — the document's own sentence, and not the package's |
| **answer at different levels** | *"What is photosynthesis?"* at `INTRODUCTORY` / `INTERMEDIATE` / `ADVANCED` / `MECHANISTIC` gives four different accounts |
| **identify what it does not know** | the level the workspace does not hold is named in the answer's limitations |
| **practise applying the concept** | the exercise selector names the item and the reason |
| **evaluate independently** | the `held-out` item is the independent test, and it is chosen last |
| **improve the knowledge representation** | six representations written from the document |
| **preserve the improvement after a forced restart** | the app was killed with **SIGKILL** and started again; the answer to *"What is the Krebs cycle?"* still begins with the imported document's sentence |

**What the journey does NOT yet show** — stated plainly, because the
mission's §16 forbids claiming more than the evidence supports:

1. the **false-premise** question — *"What is the Krebs cycle in a eukaryote
   with a mutated citrate synthase?"* — is still **answered**. The
   benchmark's two `FALSE_PREMISE` items are still answered. The entailment
   machinery is not yet wired into the question.
2. the **transfer** items are answered without carrying the conclusion.
3. the **delayed teach-back phase fails**, and is reported as failing.
4. the **consolidation** is still the unreachable W41 component: its result
   is discarded, and the compaction still hard-deletes — the mission's
   prohibition on silently destroying historical evidence is not yet
   honoured in the code.
5. the **no-consolidation baseline** the mission's §14 asks for is not
   measured.
6. the exercise selector's choices are **not yet measured against an
   alternative** (a random or a fixed order); the claim "it selects better"
   is not established.
