Three Principles

🔍 Determinism

Every decision is a verifiable Boolean chain. Identical state and input always produce identical output. No LLM in runtime.

🛡️ FROZEN layer

Ethical and domain constraints are immutable at the FROZEN layer. 6 axioms, structurally immutable, formally verifiable.

⚖️ Honesty

All claims are verifiable. No "AGI", "doesn't lie", or unverifiable numbers. Every metric traces to a measurement.

Three forms of compute

Every Boolean function is stored in one of three equivalent forms:

  • TT (Truth Table) — exhaustive table 2^k for k ≤ 20 (K_MAX)
  • CLAUSESET — DNF with Tsetlin automata for sparse, large k
  • BDD — Reduced Ordered Binary Decision Diagram for compact representation

Forms are mutually compilable; equivalence is mechanically verifiable.
